Processing (in your backend) means: taking the raw data received from the user (inputs, spending history, answers to questions) and cleaning, structuring, and computing what’s needed before sending it to the AI or database.
If you skip it, your data will be messy, inconsistent, and the AI will misunderstand context, leading to wrong or irrelevant advice.
Example
With processing:
User input:
income: "3k",  expenses: "around 2.4", date: "25/10/25"

Your FastAPI processing converts that into:
income: 3000.00,  expenses: 2400.00,  date: 2025-10-25
savings_rate = (3000 - 2400)/3000 = 0.2

Now the AI receives clean numeric values and derived features like savings_rate, so it can reason:

“You are saving 20% of your income. To reach your goal of 30%, reduce discretionary spending by 10%.”

Without processing:
AI receives messy text:
income: "3k", expenses: "around 2.4"

Model may misread “3k” as 3, 30, or 30000, and “2.4” as 2.4 JOD, not 2400. The result could be nonsense like:

“You are saving 99% of your income, you can retire early.”

Real-world parallel
Fintech systems like Plaid or Mint preprocess raw bank data to standardize currencies, detect categories (food, rent, utilities), and handle missing values. Without that, every AI insight would be chaotic.
So in your app:


Processing ensures the AI sees structured financial truth.


Without it, you’ll send garbage-in → get garbage-out, making alerts and recommendations unreliable.
